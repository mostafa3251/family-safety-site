Delete your account and data

Family Safety

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open the app and tap ⚙ Settings.
  2. Tap Privacy & my data.
  3. At the bottom, tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete my account permanently.

Your account is deleted immediately. This cannot be undone.

If you can no longer open the app

Send an email from the address your account uses to mostafafayad.cs@gmail.com with the subject "Delete Family Safety account".

We delete the account within 7 days of receiving it and reply to confirm. We ask for the same address so we know the request is really from you.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email, password and display name.
  • Your current location, route history, and place arrival/departure events.
  • Your help (SOS) requests, and check-ins and notifications linked to you.
  • Your push tokens, settings and privacy log.

What remains

  • The family itself stays for its other members; if you were the admin, another member becomes admin. If you were the last member, the family is deleted too.
  • Places you added for the family (like "Home") stay for them, no longer linked to your name.
  • Your account data (not your locations or messages) may remain in our encrypted nightly backups for up to 7 days, then it is deleted automatically.

Just want to stop sharing your location? No need to delete your account: pause sharing in Settings, or leave the family.

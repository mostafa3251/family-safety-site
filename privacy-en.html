Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Family Safety lets family members see each other's location on a map and receive arrival/departure alerts and help requests. This page explains plainly what we collect, why, who sees it, and how to delete it.

In short:
  • Only your family members in the app can see your location.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or share it with marketers.
  • We don't use the microphone, camera or contacts.
  • You can pause sharing, leave the family or delete your account at any time, and no one can stop you.

2. Who sees your data

  • Only your family members in the app see your location, route, phone status, help requests and check-ins. Families are isolated from each other inside the database itself.
  • Your privacy log is visible to you only.
  • Joining a family only happens on the person's own phone by entering an invite code, and a persistent notification is shown on that phone while sharing is on.
  • We don't sell or rent your data or use it for advertising.

3. Service providers

We use providers only to run the service; they don't use your data for their own purposes:

  • Supabase: database and accounts. Servers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering notifications to your phone (they receive the notification text and your device token).
  • GitHub: stores the encrypted nightly backup described in section 4.
  • OpenFreeMap: map images. It receives your device's IP address and the area shown on screen, not your name or account.
  • MapTiler: satellite images when you choose "Satellite". Like OpenFreeMap, it receives only your device's IP address and the area shown, not your name, account, or your family's locations.

4. How long we keep data

  • Route history and place events: 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Log of sent notifications: 30 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Messages: 30 days, then deleted automatically; you can delete your own message at any time.
  • Your latest location: erased as soon as you pause sharing.
  • Help requests: until you leave the family or delete your account.
  • Account data and privacy log: until you delete your account.
  • Backups: each night an encrypted copy of account and family data (accounts, family, places, settings and privacy log; not locations, routes, help requests or messages) is kept for 7 days so the service can be recovered after a failure, then deleted. Deleted data may remain in these copies until they expire.

5. Your controls

  • Pause sharing in Settings: your current location is erased immediately and your family sees "Sharing paused".
  • Delete your route history in "Privacy & my data".
  • Leave the family: your location, route, place events and help requests are removed from the family.
  • Delete your account immediately and permanently in the app, or by email request. See the account deletion page.

Neither the family admin nor anyone else can prevent any of this.

6. Children

The app is made for families. A child under 13 (or under the legal age in their country) should only create an account with a parent's or guardian's consent and supervision. We collect nothing from children beyond what is described above.

7. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS), and our hosting provider encrypts stored data. Access rules are enforced inside the database, not only in the app, so no family can reach another family's data. No system is perfectly secure; if we discover an issue affecting your data, we will tell you.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we'll update the date above. Any change that adds a new kind of data will be shown to you in the app for your consent before it applies.
